# AlDente Pro for Mac — Protect Your Battery Health

**AlDente Pro** is a Mac app that protects your MacBook's long-term battery health by limiting how much it charges. ## What is AlDente Pro?

AlDente Pro, from AppHouseKitchen, is a battery charge limiter for MacBooks. Keeping a laptop plugged in at full charge wears the battery down over time; AlDente prevents this by holding the charge at a percentage you set. It is a simple menu bar app with a big impact on battery longevity, and the Pro version adds features that go beyond a basic charge cap.

AlDente Pro is a native Mac app that runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## Limit charging to protect battery

The core of AlDente is setting a maximum charge level. You choose a limit — commonly around 80 percent — and AlDente stops charging there, so your MacBook is not constantly held at 100 percent while plugged in. This reduces the wear that shortens battery lifespan, keeping the battery healthier over months and years.

## Heat protection and sailing mode

AlDente Pro adds protective features. Heat protection pauses charging when the battery gets too warm, since heat also degrades batteries, and sailing mode lets the charge drift within a range rather than holding an exact number, reducing unnecessary charging cycles. Together they treat the battery gently under everyday use.
